# Privacy Policy for 字海 (Zihai) Mobile

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026  

字海 (Zihai) Mobile ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a completely offline-first Chinese dictionary and character reference utility.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Zero Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, physical location, or contact list.
- **Zero Search Query Logging:** All searches (Hanzi, Pinyin, English definitions) are performed 100% locally on your device via an embedded SQLite database. Your searches are never transmitted to any external server or third-party service.
- **Zero Device Identifiers:** We do not track or store your IP address, MAC address, advertising ID (GAID / IDFA), or unique device identifiers.
- **Zero Third-Party Analytics & Ads:** The App contains no advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, etc.).

---

## 2. On-Device Storage & Local Permissions
- **Local SQLite Database:** The dictionary database is stored locally in application-private storage on your device.
- **Vibration Permission (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback when tapping dictionary cards or buttons.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Character pronunciation utilizes the native on-device speech engine provided by your operating system (iOS Speech / Android TTS). No audio recordings are made or transmitted.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The App is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date.
