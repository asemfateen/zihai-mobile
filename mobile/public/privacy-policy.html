100% Offline · Zero Data Collection · Privacy Guaranteed

字海 (Zihai) Mobile is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our handling of user information.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Zero Personal Data: We do not collect names, emails, contacts, phone numbers, or physical locations.

2. Device Permissions

  • Haptic Feedback (Vibration): Used exclusively for tactile feedback when tapping search results and character cards.
  • Text-to-Speech (Audio): Uses your device's built-in local speech synthesis. No audio is recorded or uploaded.

3. Children's Privacy

Because no personal data is collected, the application is fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR regulations and is safe for users of all ages.
