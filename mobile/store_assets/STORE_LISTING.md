## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
字海 (Zihai) is a fast, beautiful, and 100% offline Chinese-English dictionary and character exploration tool designed for serious learners, students, and professionals.

Powered by a high-performance local database of over 100,000+ words, definitions, and Kangxi radicals, Zihai delivers instant search results without requiring an internet connection or transmitting your data.

==================================================
KEY FEATURES
==================================================

🚀 LIGHTNING-FAST OFFLINE SEARCH
• Pure offline engine: Zero latency, zero network buffering, and works anywhere—including planes, subways, and remote areas.
• Flexible query handling: Search by Simplified Hanzi, Traditional Hanzi, tone-marked Pinyin (nǐhǎo), tone-numbered Pinyin (ni3hao3), flat Pinyin (nihao or spaced "ni hao"), or English definitions.
• Smart ranking: Instantly prioritizes exact words and common HSK vocabulary above obscure variants.

📖 COMPREHENSIVE CHARACTER DETAILS
• Character Bento Card: Clear breakdown of stroke counts, HSK level ratings, and Kangxi radical classifications.
• Compound Word Decomposition: Tap any individual character within a multi-character word or idiom (成语) to instantly inspect its individual meaning, radical, and stroke count.
• Native Audio Pronunciation: High-fidelity Chinese text-to-speech powered by on-device speech synthesis.

🎨 CLEAN, MODERN & DISTRACTION-FREE
• OLED Dark Theme: Custom-crafted dark visual style optimized for readability and battery efficiency.
• Zero Ads & Zero Tracking: Completely private. No advertisements, no analytics tracking, no sign-ups, and no subscriptions.

==================================================
TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS
==================================================
• Over 118,000+ vocabulary entries from CC-CEDICT.
• Complete 214 Kangxi radical index.
• Official HSK 1–6 vocabulary level indicators.
• 100% private: All data stays strictly on your device.

Download 字海 (Zihai) today and experience the fastest, cleanest offline Chinese dictionary.
```
